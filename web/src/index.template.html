HOW IT WORKS

Stats, effects & conditions

01

Match relic colours

Match red, blue, yellow or green relics to the vessel. A white slot accepts any colour. Deep slots take Deep relics.

02

Stacking rules

Attribute bonuses add. Applicable multipliers combine. Non-stacking duplicates count once; inactive effects stay visible.

03

Calculation limits

Weapon AR includes attribute scaling. Enemy defence, attack motion values and unverified effects remain outside the damage comparison. Toggle a condition only when it applies to your fight.

How calculations, random rolls and incomplete effects are handled

Relic audit (9 November 2026): uploaded regulation data version 10110019 is earlier than the comparison snapshot 10350000. Numerical magnitudes are checked separately from activation, stacking and roll eligibility. Seven existing modifier entries remain community models and are labelled in the breakdown. Raider’s damage-during-skill bonus is a manual 20-second scenario: ×1.10 damage and ×1.20 maximum stamina. Identical fixed/random aliases of known non-stacking effects count once.

Base HP, FP and stamina agree with HP = 80 + 20 × Vigor; FP = 45 + 5 × Mind; stamina = 48 + 2 × Endurance in the retrieved character tables. Relic projections use those linear relationships, then resource multipliers, with a floor at the end. Attributes are constrained to 1–99. Resource extrapolations outside the observed attribute ranges are marked.

Every relic effect is visible, but only mapped numerical effects enter totals. Character-specific effects activate only for that Nightfarer. Conditional effects start off. Stat-reallocation effects are excluded unless their level-specific model has been verified. Omitted numerical effects make the projected status incomplete; no missing effect is treated as zero strength.

Combat figures are applicable relic factors, not weapon attack rating or final damage. Different hit types and weapon conditions cannot always apply simultaneously. Damage negation combines the remaining-damage factors. Unknown self-stacking duplicates are conservatively excluded. The separate Weapon stats models weapon scaling from source coefficients. Temporary effect timing, ammunition and enemy defence are not simulated.

Custom rolls warn about snapshot type eligibility, same-relic exclusion groups, paired drawbacks and roll-slot eligibility. These uncertain metadata checks do not block adding an owned relic or exclude its mapped effects from totals. They are planning records, not proof that a roll exists in your inventory or can drop from a particular source. Fixed relics follow their own game item records. Unique items cannot be equipped twice. Random scenes have no finite inventory catalogue: this archive lists their effect types instead. The 84 fixed records are catalogued game data, not a guarantee that every item is obtainable; The Will of Balance has unconfirmed current availability.

Forgotten Scholar’s Goblet has a reported colour conflict in community sources. This planner uses the game-data tooling’s red first Deep slot. Verify it against your inventory. Artwork is from the official character gallery and remains © Bandai Namco / FromSoftware. Independent fan project; no affiliation or endorsement.

Builds and your relic collection are saved in this browser. PC save import reads files locally; it never uploads or edits them. Sharing puts the build in the link itself; no account or server stores it. No save files are edited.

Weapon attack rating: sources, formulas and patch coverage

The roster and AR formula use CryptidTracker’s original Nightreign research. Weapon, reinforcement, element-correction and scaling-curve fields were rechecked against an author-labelled Regulation 1.03.5 community snapshot. It is a relic-weight mod distribution; its weapon fields match the original baseline across the curated roster and its 17 curves. Relic rolling weights from that mod are not used. Sixteen independent current AR reference values for Zweihander and Greatsword also match. This is versioned community data, not an official release export or live feed. Two DLC starter labels describe the character and weapon type because their exact English item names were unavailable in the source name table.

Attribute terms use correction curves and the AttackElementCorrect influence/override values, including the source’s negative-term branch. No requirement penalty is applied. Attributes follow the source model’s 1–99 range. A 1.5× Strength bonus for two-handing is not assumed.

Each channel is truncated separately; total AR truncates the sum of unrounded channels. Staff and seal strike AR is separate from casting scaling. Catalyst spell-buff values exclude spell motion values and school/passive bonuses. Bow, crossbow and ballista ratings exclude ammunition. Starting-weapon infusion relics, weapon passives, status buildup and temporary weapon buffs are outside the AR model. Use the affinity selector for a hypothetical supported weapon variant; equipping an infusion relic does not automatically choose a variant. Spell and item-only relics are excluded from weapon strike potential. Conditional and inventory bonuses require the correct run condition to be enabled.
